pub fn decode_vowel(character: char) -> Option<Vowel>
Expand description

Decodes a precomposed Vietnamese vowel character into its semantic Vowel; None for characters outside the vowel alphabet (a ă â e ê i o ô ơ u ư y with tones, đ, ASCII, …).

O(1) and table-free: a constant number of range checks pick a region, the small match arms below resolve (quality, tone), and the case comes from a single bit (cp & 0x20 for Latin-1, cp & 1 parity for the Vietnamese block and the Extended pairs). is_uppercase is never called — Unicode case is arithmetic.
